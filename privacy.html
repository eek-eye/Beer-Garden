Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what personal data Bar Chinesca Mxli collects on barchinesca.club, why we collect it, where it is kept and how you can have it deleted.

Who we are

Bar Chinesca Mxli
C. Juárez 22, Primera, 21100 Mexicali, B.C., Mexico
Phone: +52 686 364 2083

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you create an account with email and password, the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Google or Facebook sign-in: if you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that service. We do not post anything to your account and we do not get access to your friends, posts or messages.
  • Reservation details: the date, time, number of guests and table you book, your order number, and the name and email you enter on the reservation.

Why we use it

Only to run reservations: to create and manage your account, to save your table bookings, to show them on your profile, to let you cancel them, to keep two people from booking the same table and to apply the limit of two tables per account. We may use your name and email to contact you about your reservation.

Where it is stored

Your account and reservations are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service run by Google. Google's servers may be located outside Mexico. Your reservations can only be read by your own account. The seating chart only shows which tables are taken on a date, never who booked them.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. We only share it with Google as the service that stores it, or if Mexican law requires us to.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. To keep you signed in, your browser's local storage holds your Firebase sign-in session and a copy of your name, email and profile photo link. The site also uses session storage to remember which section to scroll to when you move between pages. Signing out clears the sign-in data. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the sign-in tools from Google, so those services receive your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

A reservation is removed when you cancel it. Your account and any remaining reservations are kept until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Under Mexican data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Contact us by phone or through our Facebook or Instagram page (see below).

Delete your data

To have your account and reservations deleted:

  1. Call us at +52 686 364 2083, or send a message to our Facebook page or our Instagram page.
  2. Tell us the name and email address of your account (and whether you signed in with Google or Facebook).
  3. We delete your account and all your reservations within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Bar Chinesca from your Facebook settings under Apps and websites. That stops Facebook sharing data with us; ask us as described above to delete what we already have.

Age

Bar Chinesca is a bar and night club for adults. Reservations are for people 18 years or older.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
